Universal Healthcare Access

Healthcare is a human right, not a privilege. My plan will ensure every resident has access to quality, affordable healthcare by expanding Medicaid, negotiating prescription drug prices, and protecting coverage for pre-existing conditions.

  • Lower prescription drug costs by 50%
  • Expand Medicaid to cover 200,000 more residents
  • Protect coverage for pre-existing conditions
  • Invest in mental health services

Quality Education for All

Every child deserves access to excellent public schools. I will fully fund our schools, raise teacher salaries, and ensure no student is left behind regardless of their zip code.

  • Increase teacher pay by 20%
  • Reduce class sizes in overcrowded schools
  • Expand universal pre-K programs
  • Make community college tuition-free
