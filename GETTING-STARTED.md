## Quick Start Installation

### 1. Upload Theme
- Download CampaignPress from your account
- Go to **Appearance > Themes > Add New > Upload Theme**
- Upload the `.zip` file and click **Install Now**
- Click **Activate**

### 2. Install Recommended Plugins
- Click **"Begin installing plugins"** in the admin notice
- Recommended: Contact Form 7, The Events Calendar, GiveWP, Yoast SEO, Wordfence Security

### 3. Import Demo Content (Optional)
- Go to **Appearance > CampaignPress Options**
- Click **Import Demo Content** tab
- Select your layout preference and click **Import**

### 4. Customize Your Site
- Go to **Appearance > Customize**
- Configure colors, typography, and layout options
- Add your candidate information and campaign details
