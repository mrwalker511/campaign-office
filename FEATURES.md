## 📦 Free Features

Everything you need to launch a professional campaign website.

### 🎨 Custom Gutenberg Blocks

- **Hero Commander** - Dynamic hero sections with typewriter effects, parallax backgrounds, and CTAs

- **Bulk Actions** - Update multiple volunteers, export lists
- **Opportunity Management** - Create and assign volunteer opportunities
- **Hours Tracking** - Log volunteer hours and activities
- **Shortcodes & Blocks** - Add volunteer forms anywhere on your site
- **Export Capabilities** - CSV export of volunteer data
